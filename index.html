Flagship project

FinGuard AI — compliance intelligence for banks

Most AI document tools stop at "upload a file, ask a question." That's not enough for a regulated industry. FinGuard AI adds the controls a real bank compliance team would actually need — a tamper-evident audit trail, automatic data protection, and safeguards against the AI guessing when it shouldn't.

One deep, production-shaped system — not several disconnected demos. Real engineering integrates infrastructure, security, and application logic together; this project does the same.

01

Query provenance ledger

Every question and answer is hash-chained — altering a past record breaks the chain and is instantly detectable.

02

Confidence-gated escalation

Low-confidence answers are flagged for human review instead of being presented as fact.

03

Regulatory drift detection

Automatically compares document versions and scores how risky each change is.

04

Multi-tenant isolation

Tested against a real adversarial injection attempt — isolation held.

05

PII redaction firewall

Sensitive data is stripped before it ever reaches the AI model, not after.

06

Regulatory time travel

Answers questions using the rule that was actually in effect on a specific past date.

07

Compliance score dashboard

Tracks cumulative regulatory risk over time, turning isolated events into a visible trend.
